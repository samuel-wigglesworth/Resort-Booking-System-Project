Resort Rooms & Facilities

The following facilities are available to all resort guests during their stay.

Swimming Pool

Outdoor pool with a separate shallow area for children. Towels provided at the poolside kiosk.

Open: 07:00 – 21:00

Restaurant

Full-service restaurant offering breakfast, lunch, and dinner. Menu features local and international cuisine.

Breakfast 07:00 – 10:30 | Lunch 12:00 – 14:30 | Dinner 18:00 – 22:00

Fitness Centre

Fully equipped gym with cardio machines, free weights, and resistance equipment. Personal training available on request.

Open: 06:00 – 22:00

Spa & Wellness

Treatment rooms offering massage therapy, facials, and body treatments. Advance booking required.

Open: 09:00 – 20:00

Conference Room

Air-conditioned meeting room accommodating up to 30 delegates. AV equipment and catering available.

Available on booking

Business Centre

Workstations, printing, and scanning services. High-speed wired and wireless internet access throughout.

Open: 08:00 – 20:00

Parking

Secured on-site parking for resident guests. CCTV monitored. Valet parking available at additional charge.

24 hours

Laundry Service

Same-day laundry and dry-cleaning service. Requests submitted before 09:00 are returned by 18:00.

Mon – Sat: 08:00 – 18:00
